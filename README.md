# Mechatronics and Sensor Systems

**Author:** Zahra Aslami
**Affiliation:** B.Sc. Student in Mechanical Engineering, Sharif University of Technology

## Overview
This repository focuses on the design, analysis, and simulation of analog circuits and mechatronic systems. The projects included here demonstrate the practical application of electrical engineering principles, such as sensor signal conditioning, operational amplifier circuit design, and active noise filtering.

## Projects Included
*   **NTC Temperature Sensor Design:** Design of an analog circuit for precise body temperature measurement, including op-amp signal amplification and active band-pass filtering to eliminate power-line noise.
